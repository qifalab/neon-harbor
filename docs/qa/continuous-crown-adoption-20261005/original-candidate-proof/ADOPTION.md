# Continuous near-quay crown candidate, version 3

This isolated candidate is **CPU-validated and awaiting actual High native art and performance review**. The earlier 1,728-leaf actual High image was inspected and its narrow, separated vertical lobes and empty middle crown remain an art failure. That original image, source and seal are preserved. No production / ROOT / NEXT source, build, Git state, native method or original seal was written; no browser or GPU was started.

The candidate preserves all original bark and the complete previous **1,728-leaf attribute prefix**. An independent final random stream appends another 1,728 same-sized, closed leaf blades. The 54 overlapping filled clusters comprise 12 bridges between adjacent original curved branches, 12 inner-crown clusters, 24 shoulder clusters in three staggered layers on both Z sides, and six upper-middle clusters. This populates centre and side spans rather than increasing density solely at the six old branch ends. New blades retain the original 29–43 cm length, 13–21 cm width and 1.6 mm thickness; broader natural pitch and roll variation increases visible real blade area from side views. No enlarged cards, alpha planes, crown sphere, envelope shell or new bark is introduced.

The old bark envelope stays `[-3.4, 0.3, -3.4]` → `[4.5, 12, 3.4]`. The explicitly separate foliage envelope expands to `[-3.85, 0.3, -4.2]` → `[4.5, 12, 4.2]`. The recipe and manifest expose both envelopes. All actual positions are checked against their respective envelope; original trunk, branch-root geometry and tight bark bounds stay byte-identical. This expansion changes upper foliage only; existing roots, sites, collision and world source are unchanged. Newly appended leaves mostly populate the 8–11.4 m crown layers with irregular upper edges and open lower branch lines.

**Adopt only the 13 regular files in `payload-ledger.json` under `payload/`.** Do not copy candidate execution symlinks or CPU scripts. The runtime change is exactly `maxTriangles:150000,maxTreeTriangles:25000` → `maxTriangles:270000,maxTreeTriangles:45000`. Every other runtime byte, including six sites, 80 / 100 m thresholds and ownership functions, is identical to the prior sealed payload. `runtime-budget-only.patch` records the two changed constants; the world hook is reused.

Each tree has **3,456 leaves / 43,504 triangles**, including 2,032 unchanged bark triangles and 41,472 leaf triangles. Maximum visible submission remains **six draws**, now **261,024 triangles** counting both instance slots for each variant. Three templates total **17,794,272 raw GLB bytes** and **17,227,584 decoded Float32 attribute bytes**. Compared with the previous 1,728-leaf candidate this adds **124,416 maximum submitted triangles**, **8,213,720 raw GLB bytes** and **8,211,456 attribute bytes**. These CPU byte and submission figures do not measure hardware VRAM or frame time. Native performance acceptance must account for the increased cost.

It reports actual bounds, unit normals, surface and single-top-face areas and inner-crown distribution. The three original embedded PNGs and original materials are byte-identical. `cpu-real-branch-attachment-proof.json` passes all **72 actual-parent-triangle containment checks**.
